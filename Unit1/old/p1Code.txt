Specification Example:
- Assume 32-bit address-space
- base address for mapped registers from UART port: 0x10000000
- Each register given 4 bytes, but has capacity of 1 byte

Layout:
+0 status
+4 tx_data (write)
+8 rx_data (read)

Status masks:
- 0x01 - tx_data empty
- 0x02 - rx_data ready
- ... - error codes
