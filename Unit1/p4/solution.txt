Part 0:
One of the biggest differences between threads and processes is that of which resources are shared.

Processes:
- Spawned processes inherit open file descriptors
- Separate virtual memory image

Threads:
- Share global variables, heap, virtual address space, and other process-level resources (FDs)
- Separate stacks, register states, thread-local storage, and TID
